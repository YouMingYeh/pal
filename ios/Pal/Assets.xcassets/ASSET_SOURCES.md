# Icon sources

GoogleCalendar: unmodified Google-approved artwork from https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/static/images/icons/product/calendar.svg. Used only to identify the Calendar integration. Brand guidelines: https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/getting-started/brand-your-internal-communications-with-google-workspace.

DoodlePlaces, DoodleCalendar, DoodleReminder, DoodleChat: Khushmeen Sidhu, https://khushmeen.com/icons (CC0). SVG copies from https://github.com/svatsa159/react-doodle-icons/tree/master/src/icons, converted to native template PNG assets.

PalLogo and AppIcon: user-supplied Pal artwork, 2026-10-11. AppIcon is the same artwork resized to the required 1024×1024 size.

PalMascot expressions: user-supplied `pal-mascot-handoff-v1`, rendered from its transparent SVGs. The orange and warm-white artwork is identical in light and dark appearances. The local animation player retains the Bloub MIT license in `Pal/Mascot/Bloub-LICENSE.txt`; integration provenance is in `Pal/Mascot/PROVENANCE.md`.
